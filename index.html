Better than your M8(B)

Can you beat an 8B model at GeoGuessr?

We are teaching an open 8-billion-parameter AI model (Apertus) to guess where a street-level photo was taken, as a course project at ETH. To know how good "good" is, we need a human reference.

You will see 30 photos from around the world, some of them from Switzerland, in random order and not labelled.

  1. Look at the photo. You can't move or zoom it.
  2. Click the map where you think it was taken. Zoom and pan as much as you like.
  3. See the true spot and your points, up to 5,000 per photo.

Only your first run is recorded: your nickname and your 30 pins are averaged with everyone else's into the human baseline that we compare the model against. Replays are fine but don't count, and please don't look anything up.
